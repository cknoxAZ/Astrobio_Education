About Teaching Astrobiology

A practical starting point for educators who want to connect questions about life in the universe with places, people, scientific inquiry, and human meaning.

Our purpose

This site brings together place-based education, interdisciplinary learning, humanities and ethics, active learning, and the LINCSS 5 Ps: People, Place, Personal, Practice, and Profession. It is designed to help secondary and introductory undergraduate educators find meaningful ways into astrobiology.

The Big Questions pages connect basic concepts with University of Arizona research and relevant space missions. Classroom activities are adaptable teaching sketches rather than complete lesson plans or formal standards alignments.

Contributors and sources

This collection is being developed by Dr. Corey Knox, Deputy Director of the Arizona Astrobiology Center at the University of Arizona.

Research and mission summaries link to their source organizations, including the University of Arizona, NASA, and the Lunar and Planetary Institute. Linked researchers and organizations are sources of information; their inclusion does not imply authorship or endorsement of this website.
