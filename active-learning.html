Active learning

Make students’ observations, reasoning, collaboration, and revision visible.

04

Active learning

Invite students to observe, model, deliberate, create, and revise. Make their reasoning visible through field notes, evidence maps, peer discussion, and explanations that change with new information.

Try thisPresent an ambiguous microscope sample. Students record observations, propose competing explanations, and identify the next test they would need.

Plan a learning experience

Present an ambiguous image or sample. Ask groups to propose alternative explanations and choose a follow-up test.

Collect an initial claim and a revised claim so students can explain how evidence changed their thinking.
