Question 05 / Reading worlds through light

Are there habitable worlds beyond our solar system?

An exoplanet orbits a star beyond the Sun. Researchers study planets’ sizes, orbits, atmospheres, and host stars to evaluate their environments. Finding a planet is the beginning of understanding it.

Reviewed October 17, 2026 · Secondary & introductory undergraduate

Start here.

During a transit, a planet crosses its star from our viewpoint. Some starlight passes through its atmosphere. Different molecules absorb characteristic wavelengths, but clouds and features on the star can complicate interpretation.

Starlight → atmosphere → instrument → interpretation

Research at the University of Arizona

Pandora’s Arizona connection

UA leads Pandora mission operations and contributes science under Daniel Apai. The mission began science operations in August 2026, observing stars and planets together to improve atmospheric interpretation.

Mission connection

Pandora and Webb

Pandora is in science operations; Webb is conducting astronomical observations.

Pandora helps distinguish stellar effects from planetary atmospheric signals. Webb studies exoplanets among many targets. Neither a spectrum nor a water detection alone establishes life.

Bring it into the classroom

Read a spectrum with caution

Use a clearly labeled simulated spectrum and molecular reference patterns. Ask students what they can infer, what a cloud or starspot might change, and which claim exceeds the evidence.

Evidence of learningAsk students to submit a supported claim, one alternative explanation or perspective, and a question that remains unresolved.

Suggested format: 20–30 minutes in pairs or small groups, followed by a brief written reflection. Adapt materials and complexity for your learners.

Begin with place

Compare an unresolved planetary observation with learning about a distant landscape through limited measurements. What is lost when we cannot inspect a place directly?

Humanities & ethics connection

How do artistic renderings shape expectations? Compare an illustration with a data plot and label what is measured, modeled, or imagined.

Connect the 5 Ps

Practice: interpret indirect evidence. Profession: highlight operations, software, and astronomy. Personal: invite students to choose a question about another world.

Revisit the full framework →

What we know

Evidence gives us a starting point.

Exoplanet observations reveal diverse planetary systems. Atmospheric measurements provide clues to composition; water vapor alone does not demonstrate oceans, habitability, or life.

What remains uncertain

Keep the question open.

Which nearby rocky worlds retain atmospheres? How reliably can we separate planetary signals from stellar effects, and interpret them in environmental context?
