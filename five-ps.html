The 5 Ps: framework overview

LINCSS—Linking Inclusive Narratives to Create STEM Synergy—uses People, Place, Personal, Practice, and Profession to connect scientific learning with communities, environments, lived experience, inquiry, and possible futures.

How the framework works

The 5 Ps are interconnected dimensions for designing meaningful and inclusive learning. Begin with a scientific question or existing activity, then consider how each dimension could deepen students’ connection to the topic and the work they do.

The framework is not a fixed sequence or a requirement to give every P equal space in every lesson. Choose connections that serve your learning purpose, make those connections substantive, and examine what students say, do, or create as evidence of learning.

Use it to design or revise an activity

  1. Identify the scientific idea and what students will do with it.
  2. Consider the people, places, interests, practices, and pathways connected to that idea.
  3. Select meaningful connections and revise the activity.
  4. Gather student explanations or reflections and use them to improve the design.
