Interdisciplinary learning

Build a shared investigation that needs biology, chemistry, geology, astronomy, and human perspectives. Have students explain what each discipline contributes and what remains unresolved.

Teams design a life-detection investigation. Identify the contributions of biology, chemistry, geology, astronomy, communication, and ethics.

Assess how students combine contributions into one explanation, rather than simply listing disciplines.
