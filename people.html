People

Attend to the traditions, knowledges, and practices of cultural groups and communities, including people affected by scientific work.

Ask: Whose knowledge and experiences are included?

Bring it into teaching

Invite students to examine whose perspectives are visible in a scientific story and whose are missing. Include communities affected by scientific work, with appropriate permission and context.
