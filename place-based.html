Place-based learning

Begin with nearby environments, then connect observations to larger questions about life.

01

Place-based astrobiology

Use nearby soils, rocks, water, heat, and living things to explore habitability. Connect environmental observations with the histories and relationships that make a place meaningful.

Try thisCompare a shaded and exposed microhabitat. What evidence explains differences in conditions? Where does the comparison with Mars help—and where does it break down?

Plan a learning experience

Ask students to compare two microhabitats, document the conditions, and explain one useful connection—and one limit—to a planetary analogy.

Invite community perspectives through relationships and permission. A place is more than an environmental sample.
